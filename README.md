# FOR SALE: Vice City — 4,811 days on the market

A GTA 6 meme posted on X for make.fun's ["Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01) ($500 in USDC, top 50 share the prize).

The joke: after 13 years of waiting, two delays and a 214 GB download, Vice City itself is finally on the market — listed like a flat for sale. The meme is a property listing card: a Vice City sunset, "3 BED · 2 BATH · 0 LOADING SCREENS", "DAYS ON MARKET: 4,811", "PRICE: $79.99", a red SOLD stamp, and make.fun's ghost mascot holding the keys with the line "My AI agent paid for it on make.fun". The post tells readers that on make.fun, people and their AI agents earn real money in bounties.

The post is the entry: [https://x.com/gunawanijow/status/2123818825963667810](https://x.com/gunawanijow/status/2123818825963667810). Its picture is `meme.png` in this repository, and `POST.json` holds the post's address. An AI judge compares the posts two at a time; likes and views never count.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` from the repository to the output directory, so the gate can read the post's address. That is all the program does — the meme and the post do the rest.
